## Description 
This project will develop and implement a experimental platoon control system composed by several selfdriven vehicles on a small scale using the AgileX LIMO PRO robot set. The platoon's lider can be autonomous with a predefined route or by a teleoperation, with a remote driver. The followers vehicles replicate with precision the trajetory and the lider's velocity profile using continuous comunication message exchange vehicle to vehicle(V2V). Additionally, will be approach and test security critical scenarios like hard brakes, the sudden reduction in speed, and dangerous situations in the middle of the platoon that provoque a division on the platoon(platoon split), allowing that the second group safely stops until an remote driver takes control of the situation and assumes the lidership of the sub group

## Problems
- Road safety:
    - The majority of the accidents are caused by human mistakes(fadigue, distractions)
- Operational costs
    - According to the iru (Internacional Road Transport Union), in the EU there are 502 thousand vacancies to be filled(representing about 13% of the truck driver workforce) and 660,5 thousand truck drivers will retire until 2030. So there is a structural problem with missing truck drivers. 

## Goals
- Present and demonstrate a experimental complete solution to controland security managment a platoon on a small scale
- Contribut to road sefety
